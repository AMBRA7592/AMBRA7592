AWS Solutions Architect Associate: The One-Guide Study Kit
Candidate handoff — Revision 4 — 6 October 2026

Learn AWS architecture from the ground up, through one structured resource.

1. OPEN THE GUIDE

Extract this ZIP first. Then open this file in your web browser:
    SAA-C03_Beginner_Guide_2026-10-06_v4.html

The saved HTML contains the complete guide, diagrams, questions, explanations
and timed practice controls. It works offline; you do not need a preview server,
AWS account, sandbox, lab or additional course for this study route.

The .md file is the same guide in Markdown, provided as an alternative format.
Use the HTML reader for the simplest experience. In a plain-text editor, the
Markdown answer key shows some formatting markup.

2. FOLLOW THE LEARNING ORDER

Start with section 0, the learning plan. Follow its reading order rather than
reading every chapter from front to back. Your first study session covers:
    Section 1 — Foundations and scenario reasoning
    Section 6.1 — EC2 instance basics
    Section 7.1 — Storage foundations

Read a small topic, close the guide, and explain the idea in your own words.
Draw the request flow, work through the example, then change one requirement
and explain how that changes the design.

The ten-day plan is an intensive suggested sequence. Extend it whenever a
checkpoint is still difficult; ten days is not a minimum or a completion promise.

3. USE THE INCLUDED PRACTICE

The guide includes 56 worked scenarios, four architecture diagrams, 12 written
architecture exercises and 65 original scenario questions.

When the learning plan reaches Appendix C, take its first sitting closed book
for 130 minutes. Keep Appendix D's answers and explanations closed until you
finish. Record guesses as well as incorrect answers, then review the reasoning
for every choice you misunderstood. Use Appendix E's written exercises to check
whether you can apply a concept when the requirements change.

The internal score is an uncalibrated learning score. It does not convert to
AWS's scaled exam score or predict a pass.

4. KEEP A STUDY RECORD

MY_STUDY_LOG.txt is an editable blank template. Save your own copy and duplicate
its entries as needed. Record study time, uncertain concepts, mistakes, and any
outside help. There is no automatic submission or study-log collection.

Legitimate online practice exams and their answer explanations are permitted
but are not supplied in this package. If you use them, keep first-attempt scores
separate from retakes. A concept newly taught by an external explanation should
be recorded as a guide gap, even when your answer was correct.

The guide suggests aiming for 80% on two different, previously unseen full-length
practice exams as a personal readiness checkpoint. That is not AWS's raw passing
threshold or a validated pass predictor.

5. BEFORE BOOKING

Check AWS's Associate certification page and the exam guide linked from it:
    https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-associate/

This kit targets SAA-C03. Confirm that the version you intend to sit still
matches it. The guide's source links are for fact checking, not additional
assigned study.

IF YOU ARE TAKING PART IN THE PILOT

Follow Appendix E.1. Keep this edition unchanged during the pilot. Record your
starting experience, preparation time, unseen-exam results and concepts needing
outside help. Report difficulty, unsuccessful attempts and changes to the study
route as well as successful outcomes. Your coordinator will collect feedback.
